    DGTS · Digit Span
    Direction
    Mode
    How to play

    Digits appear one at a time, a new one every 0.5 seconds, starting with 3 digits.

    • Forward: type them in the order shown. Backward: type them reversed.
    • Get it right and the next sequence is one digit longer.
    • Easy gives you a second try at each length. On Hard one miss ends the run.
    • Score = the longest sequence you got right. Use the keypad or your keyboard. Your answer submits by itself once you've typed the full length (Enter submits early).

    How to play

    A signal flashes in the middle, then the problem appears. Answer with ← → on your keyboard or the two buttons.

    • Math: ← ODD · EVEN →. Every number is under 100.
    • Arrows: press the direction most arrows point.
    • Hard: both problems appear, top and bottom shuffle. Only the one the signal picked counts — remember it.
    • Super hard: the odd/even keys sometimes swap sides. The buttons always show the current layout.
    • +1 right, −1 wrong. Taking longer than 4 seconds counts as wrong.

    CNFM · Trade Confirm
    Code length
    Speed
    How to play

    The order ticket ID is at the top. Four trade confirmations are below — only one matches exactly.

    • Tap the exact match (or press 1–4 on a keyboard).
    • Decoys differ by one character: look-alikes like 0/O, 1/I, 5/S, 8/B, or two neighbours swapped.
    • You start with 5 seconds per ticket. Every match takes 0.1s, 0.2s or 0.3s off the clock, down to 0.6s.
    • You have 3 lives. A wrong tap or running out of time costs one.
    • Score = number of matches.

    AUDT · Tape Audit

    Number logic, trading-firm style: find the next value in each sequence and pick it from 5 options. 20 questions, 5 minutes. You're not expected to finish them all.

    Example
    2, 6, 3, 9, 6, 18, ?
    Alternate ×3, −3 → 15
    Example with fractions
    13, 25, 37, 49, ?
    Term n = n/(2n+1) → 511
    How to play
    • 6 medium questions, then 14 hard ones, drawn from a bank of checked sequences. Expect fractions, negatives and operation cycles.
    • Tap an option (or press A–E / 1–5). The bottom of the screen flashes green or red. Skip sends a question to the back of the line.
    • +1 right, −1 wrong, 0 skipped — guessing costs you, so skip when unsure.
    • Check in this order: differences, ratios, odd/even positions separately, repeating operation cycles, differences of differences, primes/squares/powers, combinations of the previous two or three terms. For fractions, follow numerators and denominators separately.

    How it works
    • You buy in for 100 big blinds (or what's left of your bankroll). Your chips go back to the bankroll when you leave the table.
    • Higher stakes unlock as your bankroll grows: Riverboat at 4,000, High Rollers at 16,000.
    • Each bot's style is shown under its name — tight, loose, aggressive, calls a lot. Adjust to it.
    • When you face a bet you see the pot odds: the share of the final pot you'd be paying, which is how often you need to win for the call to break even.
